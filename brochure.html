The Basics

What Is This Tool?

This is a web-based system that helps doctors figure out which virus might be causing a patient's illness. You enter the patient's details — their age, location, symptoms, and how long they've been sick — and the system instantly tells you the most likely viral infection and which lab tests to order.

It's built on real data from over 6,63,000 clinical cases across India, so the recommendations are grounded in actual medical experience.

Who Can Use It?

Doctors, clinicians, and healthcare staff at participating hospitals. Access is available to authorised ICMR and NIE personnel.

Is It Safe to Use?

Yes. All data is secure, and only authorised users can sign in. The tool assists medical decisions — it does not replace a doctor's judgement.

Your Journey

How to Use It — Step by Step

1

Sign In

Open the app in your browser. Sign in with your email and password, or use your Google account. First-time users can create an account in seconds.

2

Go to "Test Recommendation"

This is where you enter patient details. Fill in basic information like the patient's name, age, hospital, and department.

3

Select Symptoms

Choose the patient's main health complaint (such as fever, respiratory illness, or rash) and then tick all the symptoms you observe — headache, cough, joint pain, and more.

4

Get Your Results

Press "Predict Virus" and the system instantly shows the most likely infection with a confidence score, plus the top 5 possibilities displayed in a clear chart.

5

Enrol the Patient

Save the patient's record with one click. The case is stored securely, and a unique Patient ID is generated automatically.

6

Complete the Record Later

Once lab results come back, a doctor can update the record with the confirmed diagnosis. The case status changes from "Pending" to "Completed."

Why It Matters

Better Decisions, Faster Care

When a patient arrives with a fever, there are dozens of possible causes. Ordering the wrong lab test wastes time and money — and delays treatment. This tool narrows down the possibilities using patterns from lakhs of real cases, helping doctors order the right test the first time.

It covers 30+ viral infections including Dengue, Influenza, Chikungunya, Hepatitis, COVID-19, Japanese Encephalitis, Measles, and many more — across all Indian states and seasons.
